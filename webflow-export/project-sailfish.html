The Project

Autonomous drones for search and rescue

Marine search and rescue is currently performed by specialized boats and planes. We set out to develop a proof of technology for a long range, low cost, autonomous drone to reduce the time and cost for Coast Guard and volunteer rescue to make first visual contact at an emergency site.

Is the tech there to do this?

With input and a set of requirements from Canadian Coast Guard personnel, we quickly figured out why this project was novel. Compared to drones used for land rescue, an MSAR drone faces three core challenges that no existing product addresses.

Extended Range

Search areas at sea are vastly larger than on land. The drone must achieve 160 km endurance to cover meaningful search zones.

Waterproofing

Saltwater exposure, rain, and emergency water landings demand a fully waterproof fuselage and electronics enclosure.

Harsh Conditions

Maritime weather is unforgiving. Wind, waves, and low visibility are the norm during rescue operations.

The Story

Over the past eighteen months, I founded and led a small team of students to develop the most ambitious and technically advanced project I've worked on to date.

In May 2024, I assembled a team of 6 high school students experienced in competitive robotics, software and business to create a proof of technology for a low-cost marine search and rescue drone.

With input from Coast Guard and other experts to create a set of requirements, we figured out why our project is first of its kind: compared to drones used for land rescue, an MSAR drone requires longer range, waterproofing and reliability in harsh conditions.

Testing & Validation
Motor-Battery Thrust Testing

I ran extensive motor-battery thrust tests to characterize the power system performance. These tests informed the selection of motor and propeller combinations that maximize endurance while meeting the thrust requirements for hover and forward flight. This work fed directly into my Physics IA research on motor efficiency.

Hover flight photo

Flight Testing
Hover Tuning Flights

Successfully performed short hover tuning flights to calibrate the flight controller and validate the VTOL system before attempting full transition to forward flight.

Search pattern diagram

Math IA
Search Pattern Optimization

My Math IA focused on optimizing a parametric spiral for drone search pattern considering wind and current - a mathematical model tailored to real maritime search conditions.

Video transmission diagram

Extended Essay
Video Transmission Over Water

My IB Extended Essay explored the challenges and solutions for real-time video transmission from the drone to a ground station over open water, where range and signal reliability are critical.

Fuselage CAD render

Mechanical Design
Waterproof Fuselage Design

Designed and built the waterproof fuselage from robotic submarine parts, housing the camera, battery, and autonomous flight controller in a sealed, saltwater-resistant enclosure.

Wing manufacturing photo

Manufacturing
3D-Printed Composite Wings

Agile manufacturing of 3D-printed wings from specialized lightweight polymers supported with carbon fiber, achieving the structural integrity and low weight needed for long-range flight.
